About me

I'm Yasir Rasool, from Srinagar in Jammu and Kashmir. My work sits where teaching and software meet. I care about learning that is clear, kind and honest, and about apps that respect the people who use them: no ads, no tracking, nothing a careful parent would worry about.

I also run The Khadeejah, a Kashmiri modest-wear and tailoring business, so I build for real day-to-day work as well as for the classroom.
