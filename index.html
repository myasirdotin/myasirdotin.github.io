Ethics I try to follow

These are the values I work by. They are aims, not a guarantee: every project is different, and some follow them more fully than others.

  1. Useful without the internet

    A child or a classroom may not have a good connection, so where a project allows it I make the lessons work offline.

  2. Respect for the person using it

    I avoid ads. Some projects count visits, so I can see how people find them and make them better. I try to collect only what the work needs.

  3. Clear and kind

    Plain words, small steps and a respectful tone are what I aim for, in lessons and in the apps themselves.

  4. Honest about what it does

    I try to say only what a page or an app really does, and to correct it when I find I was wrong.
