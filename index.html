How I build

Four rules I hold every project to.

  1. Works without the internet

    A child or a classroom should not need a good connection to learn. Lessons load once and keep working.

  2. No ads, no tracking

    What a learner does stays on their own device. Nothing is collected that the work does not need.

  3. Clear and kind

    Plain words, small steps, and a respectful tone. Nothing a careful parent or teacher would worry about.

  4. Honest about what it does

    No tricks to keep people tapping, and no claims a page cannot keep.
